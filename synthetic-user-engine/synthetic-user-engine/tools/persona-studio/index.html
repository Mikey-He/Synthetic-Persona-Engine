03

Set knowledge directly

Set terminology and concept knowledge directly. No experience inference.

A terminology level applies to each term in its group; it is not a random percentage. Recognizing terms and understanding concepts are independent. Changing a group clears only its specific exceptions.

04

Language & estimated vocabulary

Choose a range or enter a count. No 50-word questionnaire.

Level is always adjustable. Choosing a level switches to its Auto vocabulary default. You can then choose a different vocabulary count; the manual count takes priority.

Vocabulary automatically estimates ordinary word recognition. Technical understanding is separate; explicit terminology settings still take priority in the original engine.
